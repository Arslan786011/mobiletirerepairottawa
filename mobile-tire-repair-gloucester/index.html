Gloucester is not Orléans

Gloucester is the band of east and south-east Ottawa between Vanier and Orléans, plus the streets down toward the airport. Orléans has its own page. If you are in Blackburn Hamlet, you are in Gloucester, just before Orléans starts.

Along the river: Beacon Hill, Cardinal Heights and Rothwell Heights, off Montreal Road and Ottawa Road 174. Blackburn Hamlet is the pocket east of the Greenbelt on Innes Road. Inside the Greenbelt, Cyrville and Carson Grove sit by the 417 and St. Laurent Boulevard. South of that, Blossom Park and the Hunt Club / Albion Road area are Gloucester too.

The Ottawa airport is here, off the Airport Parkway. Hotel and parkade flats are common. Say which hotel or which level. Gloucester Centre is at Blair Road and Ogilvie Road, next to Blair station. A pin that only says "Blair" is not enough. Name the door.

Other roads to mention when you call: Innes Road, Montreal Road, Walkley Road, Hunt Club Road, Bank Street south and St. Laurent Boulevard.
