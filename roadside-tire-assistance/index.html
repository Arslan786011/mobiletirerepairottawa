What we handle at the roadside

  • Flat tire repair on the spot for nails, screws and slow leaks (more on flat repairs)
  • Spare tire installs, full-size or temporary, when a tire can't be saved
  • Blowouts and shredded tires: we get the damaged tire off and your spare on
  • Dead battery boosts if the car won't start (battery boost)

Most roadside tire problems don't need a tow truck. Fixing it where you are is usually faster, and you drive away in your own car.

Stay safe while you wait

On a highway? Pull as far onto the shoulder as you safely can, turn on your hazard lights, and stay in your vehicle with your seatbelt on. Don't try to change a tire beside live traffic.

  • Get off the road or into a parking lot if you can do it safely at low speed
  • Turn on your hazards and, at night, keep your interior light on
  • Note the nearest exit, cross street or landmark and your direction of travel
  • If you ever feel unsafe or there's a collision, call 911 first
