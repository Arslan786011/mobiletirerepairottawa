South of the airport, east of the Rideau

Riverside South is the subdivision south of the Ottawa airport, on the east bank of the Rideau. The Vimy Memorial Bridge is the link to Barrhaven. Before that bridge, the long way around was River Road or Earl Armstrong from the airport side. If your tire fails on the bridge, tell us which bank you can reach.

The homes are built along Earl Armstrong Road, Spratt Road and Limebank Road. River Road follows the river. Limebank station is the south end of O-Train Line 2, and the parking lot there is a common place to find a flat after a commute. Name the station or the shop, not only the community.

Findlay Creek is the next community east, toward Bank Street and Albion Road. Manotick is south, across Rideau Road. Leitrim Road is the older east-west road above the newer blocks. If you are past that, toward Greely, call or text first so we can confirm the trip.

A lot of Riverside South driveways are on streets that do not connect to each other except through Earl Armstrong. The cross street matters as much as the house number.
