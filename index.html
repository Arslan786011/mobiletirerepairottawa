Winter Tire Changeover in Ottawa — At Your Home

Skip the tire shop lineup this fall. We bring the tools to your driveway, condo parking or office lot and swap your summer or all-season tires for winter tires while you stay inside.

We handle winter tires already on rims (quick swap) and tires that need mounting on your existing rims, with off-rim balancing done on-site. Serving Ottawa, Kanata, Nepean, Orleans, Barrhaven and Gatineau.

7°C Rule of thumb: once daytime temps stay below 7°C, it's time for winter tires.
Dec 1 Winter tires are mandatory in Quebec (including Gatineau) from Dec 1 to Mar 15.
Ontario Insurers must offer a discount if you run winter tires. Ask your provider.
Book early The first snowfall brings a rush. Early bookings get the best time slots.

What's included

  • All 4 tires swapped at your location
  • Lug nuts torqued to your vehicle's spec
  • Tire pressure set for cold weather
  • Quick check of tread depth and tire condition
  • Mounting + off-rim balancing if your tires aren't on rims
