On rims or off rims, we do both

Winter tires already on rims

The quick swap. We take your summer or all-season wheels off, put your winter wheels on, torque the lug nuts to your vehicle's spec and set the tire pressure for cold weather.

Winter tires not on rims

We mount your winter tires onto your existing rims and balance them on-site with our off-rim balancing equipment, so there's no vibration on the highway. No shop visit.

What's included

  • All 4 tires swapped at your location
  • Lug nuts torqued to your vehicle's spec
  • Tire pressure set for cold weather
  • A quick check of tread depth and tire condition
  • Mounting and off-rim balancing if your tires aren't on rims

When to put winter tires on in Ottawa

7°C
The rule of thumb
Once daytime temperatures stay below 7°C, usually late October to mid-November.
DEC 1
Gatineau and Quebec
Winter tires are mandatory in Quebec from December 1 to March 15.

Insurance discount
