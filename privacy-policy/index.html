Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call, text, or visit our website, and how we use it.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Mobile Tire Repair Ottawa ("we", "us") provides mobile tire and roadside service in Ottawa and Gatineau. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • When you call or text us: your name, phone number, the location where you need service, your vehicle and tire details, the time of your call, and the price and details of the job.
  • When you visit this website: basic information about your visit, such as pages viewed, clicks, device and browser type, and general location (city level). This is collected with cookies and similar tools (see below).

We do not ask for or store payment card numbers on this website.

How we use it

  • To dispatch a technician, contact you about your appointment, and complete the job.
  • To send you receipts, invoices, and appointment confirmations.
  • To improve our service and our website.
  • To measure how well our advertising works (see below).

Advertising and analytics

We use these services on this website:

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads: to understand how visitors use the site and to count calls and texts that come from our ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity: to see how visitors use our pages (for example, where people click and scroll) so we can make the site easier to use.

If you contacted us after clicking one of our ads, we may share your phone number, the time of your call, and the value of the job with Google Ads. We do this only to measure which ads lead to real jobs. Google may process this information in hashed or encrypted form and uses it under its own privacy policy. We do not share your address or vehicle details for advertising.

You can limit ad personalization at Google My Ad Center, opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and read how Microsoft handles data in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Our technicians and our call centre, so they can book and complete your service.
  • Service providers that help us run the business (for example, phone, messaging, payment, and the advertising and analytics tools listed above).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store information outside Canada, where it may be subject to local laws.

How long we keep it

We keep job and contact records only as long as we need them for service, follow-up questions, accounting, and tax purposes, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (unless we must keep it by law). You can also ask us to stop using your information for ad measurement. Call or text us at (613) 601-6471 and we will respond within 30 days.
